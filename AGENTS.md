# TVM Archive — The Construct

- The site's visual direction is inspired by the Construct in _The Matrix_ (1999): an apparently infinite, high-key white void containing a small number of materially convincing analog objects. It is still TVM's archive, not an official Matrix site.
- Keep the environment free of visible walls, corners, grids, floor edges, sci-fi circuitry, green code, neon effects, and decorative interface chrome. Negative space and the contrast with the aged Radiola television do the work. Use only soft contact shadow and an unbroken white floor/background.
- Use **Times**, not Times New Roman, across page and control typography. macOS uses its installed Times; other systems use the self-hosted, open-licensed Tinos fallback. Do not distribute a proprietary Times font without a license.
- The palette is void white, soft grey, weathered brown, muted oxblood, restrained antique brass, and near-black. Typography and controls stay legible against the bright room. The header is a plain black “TVM Archive” text wordmark, not a graphic logo.
- UI copy is neutral and economical: use direct section titles, actions, counts, and factual archive context. Avoid decorative eyebrow labels, duplicate counts, promotional taglines, filler explanations, and designer credits. Preserve source provenance, uncertainty, accessibility labels, and useful failure/form feedback.
- The homepage hero fills the viewport; the transparent menu overlays it and the 2D collection starts only after scrolling. Keep the hero free of the visible introductory headline and copy so the TV has negative space (a screen-reader-only heading is fine). The Radiola's screen displays a randomly chosen catalogue recording; recordings wider than the screen letterbox onto solid black. Mouse parallax is subtle, eased, and rests head-on; touch and reduced-motion users get a stable view. Keep native playback/captions and a no-WebGL fallback.
- Keep studio lighting consistent from the first frame; delayed changes to room lighting visibly change the TV. Desktop-sized fine-pointer views load the original 2K Radiola atlas and modest anisotropy before the first TV render, then downgrade for that tab if playback is slow. Mobile/touch views begin with the 1K GLB and can upgrade after smooth playback. Data-saver overrides the desktop default. Roll back on slow frames or an ineligible resize. The GLB already carries baked AO; avoid screen-space AO halos, full-frame bloom, global color effects, and GPU-model fingerprinting. The approved CRT-Lottes adaptation belongs only on the playback screen: preserve black letterboxing, filter subpixel patterns, and disable it on constrained hardware or sustained slow playback. The approved screen emission uses four video-reactive area-light regions with subtle, smoothed illumination restricted to the separate inner-bezel mesh (`Screen_LowB`); preserve its PBR material response, exclude the cabinet/room, and disable emission before judging CRT slowdown. This is not full global illumination. Watch pages and native fallback video remain unfiltered. Keep the idle/off-screen render pause and dispose optional GPU resources on downgrade/unmount.
- `the-matrix-white-room.zip` is the supplied **source** scene. `scripts/export-construct-tv.py` selects only the Radiola TV and one playback screen, excludes chairs and source frame sequences, and exports `public/models/construct-tv.glb` plus the optional `public/models/construct-tv-color-2k.jpg`. The 1K GLB deduplicates the Radiola normal map. Change the script and re-export when altering either asset; never publish the entire Blender scene or mutate the source ZIP. Verify redistribution rights before release.
- Meta CRT (`4dlyWX`) is a light-processing reference, not a scene redesign. Keep the room, model, camera, and light placement/intensity unchanged. Apply its highlight-rolloff/ambient-response treatment only to the TV's PBR materials; preserve GGX/Fresnel, normal/roughness maps, and baked AO. Do not tone-map the footage or white void, or add motion blur, DOF, temporal ghosting, grain, vignette, or a new environment. The costly per-fragment ray prototype is opt-in only (`?screenLight=raytrace`), not the default lighting path.
- Preserve archive browsing, DVD provenance, source URLs, audio preferences, shuffle, and watch-page playback while changing presentation. Keep all routes visually consistent with the Construct, not just the hero. Do not deploy or push without approval.
